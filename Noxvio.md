Who should check with a doctor first?

Anyone who is pregnant, nursing, under 18, taking medication or managing a medical condition should ask a doctor first. Noxvio AM contains caffeine, so skip it if you're sensitive to caffeine.

Not at launch. The box includes both. If enough people ask, we'll consider selling them separately.
